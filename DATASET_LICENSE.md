Commercial use is not permitted under the application. Requests for commercial use must be submitted separately to the data provider.

Under the application terms, access is granted only to the applicant. Other researchers requiring direct access must submit their own applications. The dataset may not be transferred, resold, sublicensed, publicly uploaded, or redistributed in whole or in substantial part.

Store the dataset securely and do not make it publicly accessible. Do not share download links, credentials, or access keys.
